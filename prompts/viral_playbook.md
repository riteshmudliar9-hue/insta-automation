VIRAL PLAYBOOK (Reels for Indian small-business owners)

WHY REELS SPREAD
Instagram pushes a Reel to more non-followers when people (1) stop scrolling in the first 1-2 seconds,
(2) watch to the end or replay it, (3) share it (WhatsApp, DMs), (4) save it, (5) comment.
Write every Reel for those five signals. Usefulness wins over hype: people share what makes them look smart
to their business friends or saves them money/time.

TOPICS THAT TRAVEL (pick angles where the source supports it)
- Money: "ye galti aapka paisa kha rahi hai", cost of a manual process, a free tool replacing a paid one.
- Time: a 2-hour daily chore done in minutes (invoices, follow-ups, stock, attendance, quotations, GST data entry).
- Fear / risk: a mistake owners make with AI or data (passwords, fake AI tools, wrong GST numbers, data leaks).
- Free tools: one specific free tool + the exact job it does for a shop/office/contractor.
- WhatsApp: anything that makes WhatsApp Business orders, replies or catalogues easier.
- Before/after: the manual way vs the automated way, side by side.
- Myths: "AI se business band ho jayega" type fears, corrected with facts.

HOOK PATTERNS (line 1, max 12 words, must be specific)
- "Agar aap abhi bhi ___ manually kar rahe ho, ye dekho."
- "Ye free AI tool aapke ___ ka kaam 5 minute mein karta hai."   (only if the source supports the claim)
- "90% shop owners ___ mein ye galti karte hain."                 (only with a real number from the source; otherwise "Zyada tar shop owners…")
- "Main ne ___ try kiya. Result unexpected tha."                  (only if the source reports a real test)
- "___ ke liye ChatGPT mat use karo. Ye use karo."
- "Stop doing ___. Iski jagah ye karo."
- Open with the result, the mistake or the surprising fact. Never with "Namaste", "Hello friends" or the topic name.

STRUCTURE (15-35 seconds)
1. Hook (0-2 s): the claim/problem, plus a big on-screen text that reinforces it.
2. Proof or stakes (2-8 s): why it matters, in rupees, hours or risk.
3. The how (8-25 s): 2-4 fast, concrete steps or a before/after. New visual every 2-4 seconds.
4. Payoff + comment hook (last 4 s): the result, then "Comment KEYWORD, main aapko ___ DM kar dunga."
   The DM must deliver exactly what was promised (a checklist, the steps, the tool links from the sources).
Make the last line flow back into the first so the Reel loops naturally when possible.

HINGLISH STYLE
- Write the way a smart young Indian creator talks to business owners: natural Hindi + English mix, in Roman script.
- English for tech words and nouns people already say in English (invoice, GST, WhatsApp, follow-up, Excel, AI tool,
  customer, stock, payment reminder). Hindi for the conversational glue (aap, ye, kaise, sirf, bas, matlab, dekho,
  karna hai, ho jayega, bachega).
- Respectful "aap", never "tu". Simple everyday Hindi, no heavy/formal words (not "prayog", "suvidha", "upyog").
- Short spoken sentences. Read every line aloud in your head: it must sound like speech, not translated text.
- Numbers as digits (5 minute, 2 ghante, ₹500). Hashtags in English.

NEVER
- Clickbait that the Reel does not pay off. Invented numbers, results or "studies".
- "Follow for more", "like and share", begging for engagement other than the single comment hook.
- Slow intros, greetings, or explaining what the Reel will be about.
